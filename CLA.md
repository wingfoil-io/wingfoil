## How to sign

Signing is one comment on your first pull request. The CLA bot will post the
instructions and the exact sentence to reply with; your signature is then
recorded in
[`.github/cla/signatures.json`](https://github.com/wingfoil-io/wingfoil/blob/cla-signatures/.github/cla/signatures.json)
against your GitHub username, and you will not be asked again.

If you are contributing **on behalf of an employer**, read [§8](#8-your-employer)
first — you may need a
[Corporate CLA](CLA-CORPORATE.md) instead of, or as well as, this one.

## 8. Your employer

This is the clause that catches people out, so it is worth reading twice.

Most employment contracts assign your employer the copyright in code you write
while employed, and many extend that to code written in your own time if it
relates to the employer's business. If that is your situation, **you cannot
grant the licence in §1 on your own** — the rights are not yours to grant.

Three ways to be clear of this:

1. **Your employer signs a [Corporate CLA](CLA-CORPORATE.md).** Best where you
   are contributing as part of your job, or where colleagues may contribute
   too. It covers a named list of employees.
2. **Your employer gives you a written waiver** for contributions to Wingfoil.
   Many companies have a standard open-source contribution policy that does
   exactly this. Keep the waiver — we do not need a copy, but you may.
3. **The work is genuinely outside your employment** and your contract does not
   reach it.

If you are unsure, ask your employer before you open the pull request. We are
happy to wait, and it is a great deal cheaper than unwinding merged code later.
