# Wingfoil 9.1.0 — unreleased

**Not released yet.** Notes collect here as changes land on `main`; the
summary of what the release *is* gets written when it ships.

## Behaviour changes

**A historical `RunFor::Duration` stops at its bound.** 9.0 ran up to two
cycles past it — one marked last — and, with sparse events, however far off the
next one was. 9.1 runs a cycle exactly at `start + duration` and none after, so
a backtest's window is its window. A backtest that counts ticks under a
duration bound sees fewer. The last-cycle flush of `buffer`, `window`, `audit`,
`debounce` and `dataframe()` lands at the bound itself, whether or not anything
ticks there, and data that ends early is still flushed. Realtime runs are
unchanged. Deviation register A9 has the detail.

## Added

**A `trading` module: `wingfoil::trading::market` and
`wingfoil::trading::execution`.** The market data vocabulary moved from
`adapters::market` to `trading::market`; the execution layer, new in 9.1,
lives beside it at `trading::execution` (feature `execution`). Feature names
are unchanged. `wingfoil::adapters::market` remains as a re-export of
`trading::market`, so every 9.0 path (`adapters::market::Px`,
`adapters::market::MarketBookOps`, …) still compiles; it goes in the next
major release.

**The OMS owns its loop.** `g.oms(config, epoch).venue(&venue)`
(`execution::oms::OmsLoopOps`) opens the OMS on a `Venue`, hands back the
venue's session at once, and `.desired(..).build()` closes the one feedback cut
in the execution layer, on the request wire. A strategy reads fills, holdings and
risk off the session and wires its decisions in as the OMS's last input, with no
`feedback_loop` of its own; `wire_oms` inside a caller's `feedback_loop` still
builds the same node, and `OmsLoop::landed` is the venue's side of the cut,
the `landed` input of `AlgoOps::algo`. `testing::SimVenue::with_latency`
models the wire each way, so the order wire's `delay`s leave the strategy
graph. An `OmsLoop` dropped before `build` logs an error: an open cut is a
venue that never hears a request.

**`GraphBuilder::replay`** replays `(value, time)` rows that cannot fail, as a
historical burst source — the infallible form of `replay_results`, which keeps
taking `Result` rows for a producer that can fail part-way.
