# Wingfoil Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0**

This agreement is for a **company** whose employees contribute to Wingfoil. It
is adapted from the
[Apache Software Foundation Corporate CLA v2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/cla-corporate.txt).

Use it when contributions are made as part of an employee's job, or when your
employment agreements assign you the copyright in code your employees write.
Individuals contributing on their own account sign the
[Individual CLA](CLA.md) instead — the bot handles that one automatically, and
no paperwork reaches a human.

## How to sign

Corporate CLAs are not signed through the bot, because the person signing must
be authorised to bind the company. Email **`hello@wingfoil.io`** with the
subject `CCLA` and:

1. This document, completed and signed by someone empowered to enter into
   agreements on the company's behalf.
2. **Schedule A** — the initial list of employees authorised to contribute.

We countersign and confirm by reply. Your authorised employees will still be
prompted by the CLA bot on their first pull request; tell us their GitHub
usernames and we will record them as covered by your Corporate CLA so the
prompt clears.

Keep Schedule A current by emailing an updated list — additions take effect
when we acknowledge them.
